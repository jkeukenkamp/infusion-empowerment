The Challenge

Native American youth face high poverty and dropout rates, compounded by limited access to transportation, resources, and digital connectivity.

  • 1 in 3 youth live in poverty.
  • 29–36% dropout rates between grades 7–12.
  • Limited access to extracurriculars, tutoring, and technology.

Who We Serve

Working Hand-in-Hand with Tribal Partners

Infusion Empowerment collaborates directly with Tribal entities to locate youth who need assistance on and off reservations, ensuring support respects sovereignty and local priorities.

We currently partner with Tribal communities across the Western United States to help remove financial and logistical barriers to art, music, sports, education, and digital learning.

  • Arizona: Apache Tribe
  • Nevada: Washoe Tribe
  • Oregon: Klamath Tribes
  • Washington: Makah Tribe
  • California: Hupa, Yurok, and Karuk Tribes

By working together, we help cover art and music class registrations and supplies, instruments, sports registrations and equipment, and transportation costs — so cost and distance are no longer barriers.

Program Pillars

Culture, Creativity, Sports, and Digital Futures

Our programs combine cultural enrichment with modern skills development, supporting youth mental health, confidence, teamwork, and career readiness.

Pillar One

Cultural & Creative Empowerment

Art and music enrich the mind, improve mood and focus, and provide powerful stress relief.

  • Art, music, and cultural class registration fees.
  • Instruments, art supplies, and materials.
  • Digital art and music production workshops.
Pillar Two

Sports & Wellness

Sports support mental, social, emotional, and physical health — and teach discipline and teamwork.

  • Sports registration fees and league costs.
  • Uniforms, shoes, and equipment.
  • Support for transportation to practices and games.
Pillar Three

Digital Learning & Workforce Development

Technology opens doors to information, careers, and entrepreneurship — while staying rooted in culture.

  • Google Career Certificates and coding workshops.
  • Partnerships with colleges and tech companies.
  • Virtual art, music, and STEM learning options.
Pillar Four

Access, Tutoring & Support

We remove barriers that keep youth from participating fully in school and enrichment programs.

  • Laptops, tablets, and hotspots to bridge the digital divide.
  • Transportation assistance on and off reservations.
  • Tutoring and academic support to reduce dropout rates.
How It Works

From Tribal Partnership to Pathways for the Future

Our mission is to break cycles of poverty and disconnection by combining cultural enrichment with modern skills development and direct financial support.

Tribal Partnership & Outreach

We collaborate with each Tribal entity to identify Native youth in need of support, respecting local priorities and cultural context.

Individualized Support

We help cover program fees, supplies, instruments, sports gear, and transportation — tailoring support to each youth’s needs.

Long-Term Pathways

Youth gain confidence, mental wellness, and skills, then connect to digital learning, workforce programs, and career exploration that open doors for the future.
